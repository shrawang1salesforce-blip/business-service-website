Before you begin

A few things
worth knowing.

Practical details about taking part, getting started and finishing your track.

Is the internship free?

The official site says the internship program has no application fee. Check the application page for current details before applying.

Is the internship virtual or offline?

The program is described as 100% virtual, so you can take part remotely with your own device and internet connection.

How long does the internship last?

The listed duration is generally 4–6 weeks. The exact schedule is provided in the offer letter.

Will I receive an internship certificate?

Interns who successfully complete the required activities and program requirements are eligible for a certificate.

How do I get access to the intern dashboard?

Dashboard access is for candidates who have completed the official application process and received an offer or confirmation letter.

What if I miss my assigned tasks?

Task submissions and participation matter. The official FAQ notes that missing deadlines may affect internship completion and certificate eligibility.

Does attendance matter?

The official FAQ says attendance is marked based on submission of the daily assigned task.

Where can I check current program details?

Program options and application information can change. Visit Veda Technology’s official application page for the latest details.

Is Veda Technology MSME registered?

Veda’s official FAQ says the company is registered under the MSME/Udyam framework. Refer to its official site for current registration information.
