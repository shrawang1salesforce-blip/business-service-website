04 A few useful answers

Good questions
to start with.

What does Veda Technology do?

Veda Technology works across technology programmes, training, internships, and IT and digital services. The best place to start is with what you’re hoping to learn or get done.

Do I need experience to ask about training?

You can ask about training whatever your starting point. Share what you already know and what you’d like to be able to do, and check which options are currently available.

How do I find out about internships?

Reach out to ask whether internship opportunities are open and what the current application process involves. Availability and requirements can vary.

What kind of digital services are available?

Services depend on the work you need help with. Describe the challenge or project you have in mind so Veda can confirm whether it’s something they can support.

Where can I find programme dates and fees?

Dates, fees and availability aren’t listed here because they can change. Please confirm the latest details directly with Veda Technology before making plans.
